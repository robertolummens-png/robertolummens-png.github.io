A Bit About How I Work

When I communicate, I don't care about fancy filler words or textbook grammar rules. I type fast, get straight to the point, and speak the natural shorthand language of someone who just wants to fix the problem. My English vocabulary for tech concepts is incredibly sharp.

🌙 The Late-Night Focus

I do some of my absolute best thinking and building late at night. When the house is quiet, I get completely locked into a technical puzzle—like a broken server connection or a network error—and I don't give up or walk away until it's completely working.

I learn by doing, skipping the boring theoretical slide decks to build real, functional setups from scratch.
